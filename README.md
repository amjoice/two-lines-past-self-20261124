## The entry

> When Dad says 'stay for dinner, the roads are icing over' on Christmas Eve, don't laugh it off and drive home anyway.
> You spend the next eleven years visiting a headstone instead of a kitchen table, and every December since has tasted of salt.

One specific moment — a spoken sentence, a place, a decision — and one sentence tracing what changed because of it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`). Stdlib only, deterministic, no network.
